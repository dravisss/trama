# Trama landing — direction and cinematic revision

## Scope

Persuade, through an interactive demonstration. Facilitators, consultants,
educators and researchers should understand what the tool does and try it.
The user selected Atlas vivo after considering Fios de uma história and Mesa de
investigação. The selected route preserves the app's paper/forest identity and
keeps causal reading legible. A new concept-selection round is unnecessary.

The public domain and hosted app are a later phase; MCP is also later. The
shipping action now opens the real, in-page experiment. No account, fake signup,
installation promise, or unimplemented hosted-editor link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A visitor discovers why a response to a delay can reinforce the delay.
The live map is the main visual and the first evidence of the product.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper (#f5f3e9), forest ink (#233e32), sage and terracotta accents,
hairline rules, Noto Serif display and Noto Sans controls. Tactile paper-craft
illustrations identify variables. Rounded, quiet controls surround an unboxed
editorial atlas; a forest-green still life provides a deliberate contrasting act.

STORY: Recognize a recurring problem; follow five causal beats; explore the
connections; edit a private session copy; download that map if useful.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 104px masthead, large left-aligned editorial question, and a
six-variable illustrated SVG atlas derived from the real model. Each illustration
opens the corresponding variable in the real demo. At 1280 × 720, the complete
cycle remains visible in the opening. At 390 × 844, question and CTA precede
the illustrated atlas. The real CLD stage then owns a full-width sticky chapter
on desktop and an unpinned manual reading flow on mobile.

FORM: Atlas vivo, user-selected in the conversation. No seed key: established
visual world, already confirmed surface direction; context launcher unavailable.
Use rendered desktop/mobile composition studies and the actual graph engine.

Process deviation: no concept-seed command or approval board was run before
the original implementation. There is no seed receipt and no user-approved
workflow waiver. We will not invent a retroactive key or claim procedural
compliance. This revision follows the user's explicit correction of an existing
candidate — illustrated loops, stronger animation and cinematic progression —
and retains the paper/forest direction. Its actual render and behavior are
reviewed independently; that review does not erase the earlier workflow gap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Reference deconstruction

Local reference: docs/images/trama-present.png, existing styles.css and
src/themes/matcha.js. Keep paper/forest palette, serif/sans contrast, curved
signed edges, semantic emphasis and guided reading. Change the proportions:
larger readable graph labels, a question-led opening and less application chrome.
No external reference controls composition. The first candidate, with text-only
nodes and a restrained two-column widget, was explicitly rejected by Ravi as
mediocre and insufficiently cinematic. That rejection is the primary visual
authority for this revision. It calls for beautiful imagery, SVGs, animation,
scale and a cinematic progression, preserving the real product demonstration.

Six existing illustrations from assets/demo/sobrecarga-filas identify the real
variables. A new ImageGen paper-loop still life appears in the audience section.
Three authored SVG studies explain creation, feedback and presentation. Asset
provenance and the complete generation prompt live in landing/assets/README.md.

## Signature and supporting motion

Signature: native scrolling selects five real Presentation V2 beats. Camera and
focus reveal the relation, extend the chain, and finally return to the complete
reinforcing cycle. Manual controls release the pin and suspend scroll following;
editing can never be overridden by the scroll conductor. Full motion ownership,
reversal, touch and reduced-motion behavior: production/motion-score.md.
Supporting motion: path drawing, short editorial entrances and text handoffs.
Reduced motion resolves the camera immediately and removes the pinned track.
No autoplay, scroll hijacking, synthetic data claims, or external library for scroll.

## Interaction boundary

Five Presentation V2 beats; free exploration; select a variable or relation;
edit its wording; add a variable and relation; drag; undo; reset; download JSON.
Model edits live in memory. Returning to the guided story uses its original map;
the visitor's draft is retained until reset or reload. Reload begins cleanly.

## Acceptance

Real engine, valid cycle and V2 references; all controls work; semantic graph
alternative; keyboard operation; reduced motion; static/no-JS reading; no shared
persistence requests; no console errors; no clipping at desktop/tablet/mobile;
all promised actions have real effects. Review is local, not deployment proof.
