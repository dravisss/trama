# Trama

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Facilitators and consultants examining organizational situations; educators and
researchers explaining systems and discussing causal hypotheses with others.

## Product Purpose

Create causal-loop maps, inspect feedback, and present an authored causal story
using the same map. Make an interpretation visible, discussable, and revisable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The reusable CLD engine supports model validation, signed relations, curated
cycles, editing, and Presentation V2. The existing application stores projects
locally in SQLite and exports standalone presentations. A causal map is not a
quantitative simulation or proof of causation.

The user intends a publicly usable hosted application at trama.org-agents.work
and an MCP integration. Both follow the landing-page work and are not current
availability claims. Multi-user persistence and public deployment remain open.

## Brand Commitments

Trama is the approved name. The landing uses the approved Atlas vivo direction:
editorial clarity and a real, interactive demonstration with finished-product
craft on desktop and mobile. Voice is direct, thoughtful Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

The actual CLD engine and Presentation V2, the illustrative Sobrecarga de Filas
seed, and existing local Noto fonts. No customer outcomes or testimonials have
been supplied. The landing example is explicitly illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate the product through its real engine.
- Keep model and authored Presentation separate.
- Preserve the distinction between a causal hypothesis and an observed result.
- Keep a visitor's experiments isolated from other people's work.
